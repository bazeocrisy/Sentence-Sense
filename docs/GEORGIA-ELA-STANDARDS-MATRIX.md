## Prerequisite review

Complete thoughts, commands with an understood you, basic capitalization, ending marks, common/proper nouns, and regular forms are prerequisite instruction supporting the current Grade 3 syntax and GUM expectations. The general standard `3.L.GC.1` is used where a discrete earlier-grade row is not represented in the retrieved Grade 3 list. No new GUM row numbers were invented.

## Scope limits

This is a grammar and sentence-writing system. It does not claim to teach every Grade 3 ELA domain (decoding, fluency, cursive, the full reading-literature curriculum, research synthesis, or poetry). Unmapped relevant writing expectations and the breadth-validation status of GUM rows above are release-review gaps.
