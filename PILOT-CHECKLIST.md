# Pilot checklist and observation log: Build 1.9.0 with Chris

Scope is frozen. The pilot's job is to watch, not to fix things mid-session. Write down what happens. Changes come after we review this log together.

## Before the session (5 minutes, without Chris)
- [ ] The preview is running and the footer says **Build 1.9.0** (see `PREVIEW-WINDOWS.md`).
- [ ] You've done the five-minute run-through in `PARENT-REVIEW-SCRIPT.md` yourself.
- [ ] **For grown-ups → Delete all saved writing**, so Chris starts fresh.
- [ ] Choose a device and write it in the log. Use the same browser for the whole pilot, since saved work stays in that browser.
- [ ] Sound on, if you want to try the optional read-aloud.
- [ ] Plan a short session, about 10–15 minutes. Stop sooner if he's done.

## During the session
- [ ] Say only: "This is a new writing part. Try it, and tell me what you think." Let him find **Start writing** himself.
- [ ] Don't help unless he asks or is stuck for about 30 seconds. Then point to **Help me** first.
- [ ] Note the exact words that confused him, and where he hesitated.
- [ ] Partway through, ask him to press **Home**, then come back. Does he find **Continue my sentence**?
- [ ] If he wants another, let him write a second sentence. Don't push for it.

## Right after (5 minutes)
- [ ] Open **For grown-ups** together. Read both versions aloud.
- [ ] Ask him:
  - What was easy?
  - What was confusing?
  - Did you feel you had to change your sentence?
  - Was Help me helpful?
- [ ] Write a parent note on each piece in For grown-ups.
- [ ] Fill in the log below.

## Stop or flag immediately if
- He gets upset by a message, or reads one as "wrong" when his writing was fine. Copy the exact message.
- Work is lost: after Home, Back or a refresh, his words are missing.
- A button or screen doesn't respond.

## After 2–3 sessions: questions for the next-milestone decision
- Can he write and check one sentence on his own in one short session?
- Are his Check answers usually accurate? If not, which part — subject, predicate, or sense?
- Does he revise, keep, or seem unsure what revising means?
- Which help rung does he actually reach?
- Did any message read as grading or pressure?
- What does he ask for next: more sentences, longer pieces, a different kind of help?
