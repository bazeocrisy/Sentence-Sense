# Five-minute parent review: Build 1.9.0, Write a Strong Sentence

Start the preview as described in `PREVIEW-WINDOWS.md`. Use a fresh browser profile or a private window so you start with no saved writing. Check that the footer says **Sentence Sense — Build 1.9.0**. Record what you see in `PILOT-CHECKLIST.md`.

## 1. Independent writing (about 1 minute)
1. On Home, the top card says **Next for you · Write a strong sentence**. Press **Start writing**.
2. Pick **An animal doing something**.
3. Type a complete sentence without opening any help, for example `My dog chased a squirrel up the tree.` Press **This is my first draft**.
4. On **Check your sentence**: tap *My dog*, tap *chased…*, then answer **Yes**. Press **Next**.
5. On **Revise if you want to**, leave the sentence unchanged. The grey box should say the app didn't notice any marks, and the button should read **Keep it and finish**. Press it.
6. **Expect:** both boxes show the same sentence, the new one is labelled "kept the same", and the praise says keeping it was a writer's choice. Nothing says "correct", and nothing pushes you to add words.

## 2. Asking for help (about 1 minute)
1. Press **Write another sentence** and pick **A food you love**.
2. Press **Help me** four times. Check the order: a question, a strategy with a word bank, an example about a *kite* (not food), then a sentence frame.
3. Confirm the box is still empty until you press **Put this frame in my writing**.
4. Clear the box. Open **Sentence starters** and tap one. Open **Word bank**, put the cursor somewhere, and tap a word. Your existing words should stay where they were.
5. Finish a short sentence, for example `pizza is my favorite`, and press **This is my first draft**.

## 3. Revising (about 1 minute)
1. On **Check**, try **It doesn't say yet** or **Not yet** if you like. The answers are recorded and nothing blocks you. Press **Next**.
2. On **Revise**, the box should ask you to look at the first letter and the end mark. These are prompts to look, not errors.
3. Open **Want to add a detail? (You don't have to.)** and read it. Change the sentence, for example to `Pizza is my favorite food.` The look-at notes should clear and the button should change to **I'm finished**.
4. Optionally press **Go back to my first draft**, confirm, and see the original return. Then type your revision again.

## 4. Leaving and resuming (about 1 minute)
1. Before finishing, press **Home**. The top card now says **Continue my sentence**. Press it and you should be back on Revise with your words.
2. Refresh the page and press **Continue my sentence** again. Nothing should be lost.
3. Press **Back** twice to see **Your first draft**, which can't be edited. Press **Next** twice, then **I'm finished**.

## 5. Reviewing both versions in For grown-ups (about 1 minute)
1. Press **Show a grown-up** (or go Home and press **For grown-ups: review saved writing** at the bottom).
2. For each piece, check the prompt, **First draft**, **Revised version**, **Changes**, **Child's own answers (not checked by the app)** and **Help used**.
3. Type a parent note, refresh, reopen, and confirm the note is still there.
4. Open **Georgia standards for each skill**. Standards codes appear only here now, not on Chris's screens.
5. When you're done, **Delete all saved writing** clears this device.

## What to watch when Chris uses it
- Does he find **Start writing** without help? Does he understand "first draft"?
- On **Check**, does he tap the right kind of words? (The app doesn't judge this. You do.)
- Does he feel he *must* change something? Keeping a good sentence is allowed.
- How long does one sentence take? The required path is 5 taps plus typing (Start writing, a prompt, first draft, Next, Keep it and finish). Answering the three check questions adds a few more.
- Note any word or button that confused him. That should guide the next milestone.

Saved writing stays only in the browser you used. A local preview (`127.0.0.1`) and the live site keep separate data.
